About Me

Hi there! I am Michael Lavin. Welcome to my website! I am from West Milford, New Jersey. I currently work at Ringwood Public Schools as an Assistant Technology Coordinator. In addition to work, I am a huge runner. I enjoy filming, editing, and posting my running journey on social media.

My first introduction to Computer Science was in 8th grade. I hated it (a little ironic now isn't it?). Instead of pursuing computer science throughout highschool, I focused on Mathematics. I really excelled in the subject, and declared as a Math Major at Villanova University in the Fall of 2021. During my first semester, I was placed in a Computer Science course and this time around I thouroughly enjoyed it. Things just made more sense this time around. After that semester, I immediately added Computer Science as a second major. The course load was difficult and strenuous, but I never backed down from that challenge.

Another huge chapter in my short college career was my time with Villanova Club Swimming and Villanova Club Volleyball. Both clubs were an oppurtunity to meet my long time friends, continue playing sports that I loved, and a way to further push my limits other ways than just educationally at university.

The summer after my junior year, I had the oppurtunity to work an internship at New Jersey Manufacturers Insurance as a part of the treasury department. I worked on various projects throughout honing my skills both technically and professionally. I capped off the summer with a presentation to high-level managment regarding various agent based commercial lines marketing strategies with a couple of my fellow interns.

My senior year at Villanova consisted of still taking advvanced courses in Mathematics and Computer Science. Such courses included, my senior capstones where I presented co-authored research paper on the complicated world of quantum computing, and an agentic self learning computer program designed to play the classic game of snake. Check out my projects page to see more.

Upon graduation in the Spring of 2025, I was excited to start my career and finally utilize the skills and knowledge I had gained. However, with the rise of Aritficial Intelligece and Machine Learning, the opportunites for Entry-Level computer science positions had become scarce. The job search was rather demotivating and dissappinting. Applications turned into rejection emails again and again. I was lucky enough to land a job with Ringwood Public Schools as an Assistant Technology Coordinator in January 2026.

At Ringwood, I am responsible for maintaining and supporting technology infrastructure throughout the district. I assist students and staff alike with various technology related issues varying from chromebook repair, to network connectivity problems, to Windows troubleshooting. In addition to the daily tasks, I have also taken on additional responsibilities regarding frontend website deveopment, board meeting audio/visual support, and IT certificate training.
